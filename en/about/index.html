30 years of engineering trust — meeting the demands of a new era

AEEC was founded in 1994 out of the need to eliminate failures at, and to repair, test and rebuild, the power facilities of the oil and gas industry. Today we deliver engineering and consulting solutions for the energy, industry, infrastructure and public utility sectors.

Our success story

An engineering school born in a difficult time

AEEC's success story began during a period of severe trial for Azerbaijan's oil and gas sector, with identifying the problems that had arisen in SOCAR's power system and applying engineering solutions to them.

At a time of acute shortage of funding and investment, AEEC made an important contribution to improving the reliability of power facilities, eliminating failures, reducing technical losses and strengthening the resilience of power supply.

Later the company built its own school of engineering and consulting in the reconstruction of the power system, energy reforms, international advisory services, certification, accountability and human capital development.

Practical engineering

Experience built on solving real production problems for SOCAR and the oil and gas sector.

Professional knowledge

Power system reconstruction, energy efficiency, system planning and technical reliability.

International consulting

Work with international financial institutions, accountability and a project management culture.

History

Key milestones

1994

A start in power engineering

The engineering firm “Mehri-2”: emergency, repair and testing services at power facilities.

1996

Research and consulting

“RW Engineering and Consulting”: advice on planning, forecasting and energy reforms.

1998

Merger

The two companies merge to form “Shems Energy” LLC.

2005

AEEC LLC

In June the company is re-established under the name AEEC and expands in the public and private sectors.

2015–2020

Regional context
