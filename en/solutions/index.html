01 · Energy Engineering & Consulting

Energy Engineering & Consulting

Installation, testing, adjustment, repair and technical supervision of 0.4, 6, 10, 35 and 110 kV networks; strategic consulting for the energy sector.

  • Technical audits of power networks
  • Modernisation of substations and lines
  • Relay protection and automation
  • Testing of cable lines and fault location
  • Energy audits and saving programmes
  • Tariffs, demand forecasting and sector reforms
  • Short-circuit and overload calculations
  • Diagnostics and upgrading of switchgear

02 · Digital & AI Solutions

Digital manufacturing and data analytics

Digital transformation of industrial enterprises: design, automation, data acquisition, monitoring and forecasting.

  • CAD/CAM-based industrial design
  • 3D modelling and rapid prototyping
  • Industrial IoT sensor systems
  • RIDS — remote reading and diagnostics
  • Predictive maintenance
  • Efficiency dashboards

03 · Green Energy & E-Mobility

Green energy and electric transport

Design, installation, maintenance, monitoring and consulting for the energy transition.

  • Design of EV charging stations
  • Maintenance of electric buses and scooters
  • Photovoltaic and wind systems
  • Energy storage, second-life batteries
  • Smart meters and microgrids
  • V2G and V2H pilot projects
