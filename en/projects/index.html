World Bank

Review of the existing power and gas networks in Azerbaijan

Technical needs assessment of the power and natural gas infrastructure, with recommendations for rehabilitation.

Strategies for the organisation, operation and development of the electricity and gas utility sector.

Commercial assessment of biomass utilisation in two regions and the investment needed.

Azerbaijan Energy Assistance Project

Restructuring of utility companies, regional gas and electricity trading strategy, tariff methodology.

Associated petroleum gas flaring — a four-country study

Study of the Garadagh Gas Processing Plant

Technical assessment of the LNG and LPG production facilities, market assessment and commercial recommendations.

Natural gas and electricity consumption forecast

Near-term and long-term electricity and natural gas projections for Azerbaijan.

Green for Growth Fund

Staff training and energy audit services

Identification of priority energy efficiency measures and investment needs for beneficiary companies.
